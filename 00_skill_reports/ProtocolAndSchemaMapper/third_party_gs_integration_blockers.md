- GameClientBuilder is blocked until math package, art plan, and final runtime lane inputs exist.

- WalletAndLaunchTester is blocked until a build/registration path and safe local secret fixture/reference exist.
- Release-to-players is blocked until all later skills and RTP/release audit pass.

## Not Blocking MathModelDesigner

MathModelDesigner can run with limits because RTP targets, volatility, bet range, coin ladder, and protocol boundaries are known. It must not claim final runtime ownership or release readiness.
